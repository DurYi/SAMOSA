## 🗂️ Data Organization

All datasets should be placed in the `data/` folder, following the structure below:

```text
data/
├── Anti-UAV300/
│   └── test/
├── Anti-UAV410/
│   └── test/
├── Anti-UAV600/
│   └── validation/
├── DUT/
│   ├── Anti-UAV-Tracking-V0/
│   └── Anti-UAV-Tracking-V0GT/
├── LaSOT/
│   ├── LaSOT_extension_subset/
│   └── LaSOTTesting/
├── OTB100/
│   └── raw/
├── TrackingNet/
│   └── TEST/
│       ├── anno/
│       └── zips/
...
```
